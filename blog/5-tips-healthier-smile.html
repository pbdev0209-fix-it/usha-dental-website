5 Tips for a Healthier Smile

A healthy smile takes more than just brushing twice a day. Here are five simple habits Dr. Usha Sribollineni recommends to her patients at Comfort Dental in Overland Park to help keep your teeth and gums in great shape between visits.

  • Brush for a full two minutes, twice a day. Most people brush for less time than they think. Try using a timer until it becomes a habit.
  • Do not skip flossing. Flossing removes plaque and food particles from between teeth that a toothbrush cannot reach, helping prevent cavities and gum disease.
  • Limit sugary and acidic drinks. Frequent exposure to soda, juice, and sports drinks can wear down enamel over time.
  • Do not use your teeth as tools. Opening packages or bottles with your teeth can lead to chips and cracks.
  • Keep up with regular checkups. Routine cleanings and exams help catch small issues before they become bigger, more costly problems.
