What to Expect at Your First Visit

Starting care with a new dentist can feel a little intimidating, so here is a simple overview of what your first visit with Dr. Usha Sribollineni at Comfort Dental in Overland Park will typically include.

  • A warm welcome and paperwork. You will fill out a health history form so the team understands your dental and medical background.
  • A thorough exam. Dr. Sribollineni will examine your teeth, gums, and bite, often supported by digital X-rays to check for issues not visible to the eye.
  • A professional cleaning. In many cases, your first visit includes a cleaning to remove plaque and tartar buildup.
  • A conversation about your smile goals. Whether it is general maintenance or cosmetic concerns, Dr. Sribollineni will listen and help build a plan together with you.
  • A clear next-steps plan. You will leave knowing exactly what, if anything, needs follow-up, and when to schedule your next appointment.

We recommend arriving a few minutes early to your first appointment, and bringing a photo ID along with your insurance information if applicable.
