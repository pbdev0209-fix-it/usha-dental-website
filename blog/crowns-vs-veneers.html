Crowns vs Veneers: What is the Difference?

Crowns and veneers are both popular restorative and cosmetic options, and patients often ask Dr. Usha Sribollineni which one is right for them. Here is a simple breakdown of the two.

Dental Crowns

A crown covers the entire visible portion of a tooth above the gum line. Crowns are typically recommended when a tooth is significantly damaged, decayed, cracked, or has had a root canal, since they provide full-coverage strength and protection.

Veneers

A veneer is a thin shell, usually made of porcelain, bonded only to the front surface of a tooth. Veneers are primarily a cosmetic solution, used to improve the shape, size, or color of teeth that are otherwise structurally healthy.

Which One Is Right for You?

In general, crowns are best for strength and structural repair, while veneers are best for cosmetic enhancement of healthy teeth. Dr. Sribollineni will evaluate your specific teeth and goals during a consultation to recommend the option that best fits your needs.
