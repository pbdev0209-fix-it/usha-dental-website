Meet Dr. Usha Sribollineni

Dr. Usha Sribollineni, DDS is a licensed dentist in Kansas practicing at Comfort Dental in Overland Park. She holds a Doctor of Dental Surgery degree along with a Master's degree (MDS) in Prosthodontics, giving her particular expertise in crowns, bridges, dentures, and implant-supported restorations. Dr. Sribollineni is passionate about restorative and cosmetic dentistry, and is committed to helping every patient feel comfortable, informed, and confident in their smile.
