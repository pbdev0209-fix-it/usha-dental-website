Meet your dentist

Care that treats you like a person, not a chart

Dr. Usha Sri Bollineni is a Kansas-licensed dentist practicing at Comfort Dental in Overland Park. Alongside her Doctor of Dental Surgery degree, she holds a Master's degree (MDS) in Prosthodontics — the dental specialty devoted to restoring and replacing teeth.

That training shows up in the details: crowns matched to the exact shade of your enamel, dentures that fit the first time, and honest conversations about what your teeth actually need — and what they don't.

  • Particular expertise in crowns, bridges, dentures and implant-supported restorations
  • A calm, unhurried approach for anxious patients
  • Plain-English explanations — see the weekly blog she writes for patients
